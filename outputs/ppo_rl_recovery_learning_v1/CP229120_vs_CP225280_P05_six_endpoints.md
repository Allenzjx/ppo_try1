## Earliest differences already visible in this bounded window

At P05 age 2 s, source N is identical for all twelve channels. The failure run's mapped FR knee baseline is 45.924 versus 45.302 degrees (+0.622); its FINAL is 39.586 versus 35.355 (+4.231), so the FINAL-minus-own-mapped-baseline difference contributes another +3.609 degrees. This is a logged composite policy/mapper execution comparison, not an independently recomputed nominal subtraction. The measured FR knee differs by +4.251 degrees. FR hip FINALs are nearly equal (5.458 versus 5.629), but measured hips already differ (14.321 versus 10.455). Thus the other supporting front leg is already materially different before FL loses top-height clearance.

FR hip response then separates much more: failure-run actual-minus-FINAL errors are +8.863, +12.143 and +26.572 degrees at ages 2/4/6 s; the retained probe gives +4.826, +2.130 and +6.207. FR remains actual TOP bearing in all six observations (about 12.18–12.78 N); RL remains GROUND bearing (13.84–14.78 N), and RR GROUND bearing (1.58–2.54 N). This is not a missing-contact explanation or proof of an illegal command: under-load tracking and coupled geometry are a priority candidate, not an established sole cause.

At age 2 s, RL wheel FINAL is +0.166 versus +0.344 rad/s despite identical +0.300 source requests; actual speeds are +0.337 versus +0.421. FR wheel FINAL is +0.316 versus +0.277, RR +0.241 versus +0.166. By age 6 s the failure run commands RR +0.243 but reads +0.082, versus probe +0.167 / +0.180. All four wheels have nonzero actual angular velocity in all six observations. FL is AIR in all six, so its negative spinning wheel is not ground traction; its less-negative failure-run speed is not by itself the cause of lost clearance.

At the first sampled age 2 s, failure-run CoM is already 8.285 mm lower; at age 6 s it is 33.095 mm lower and FL top gap is 74.020 mm worse. FR knee command/baseline separation and FR hip under-load tracking divergence deserve a specific diagnostic before prescribing another generic AUX increase. The six endpoints establish the earliest difference sampled here, not the true first divergence since P01; different weights, runtime versions and entry histories prevent single-factor causal attribution.
