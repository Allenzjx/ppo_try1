## What actually enters reward

- `semantic_transfer_roles.py:154–178`: for RL the receiver is FR. Direction is normalized **window-start FR wheel center minus window-start mass-weighted CoM**, held fixed within each physical window. Current configuration uses a sliding 0.5 s window, 1 mm displacement scale, 5 mm/s velocity scale, and 1/15 s evidence maturity (`stage_task_spec.yaml:33`). `directional = max(clip(dot(delta_COM,d)/0.001), time_credit*clip(dot(COM_velocity,d)/0.005))`. There is **no independent same-sign lateral-y requirement**. Thus sufficiently positive forward contribution can outweigh opposite lateral motion and saturate this directional component.
- This is not the complete transfer score: `measured_transfer=max(directional, clipped target-leg normalized-load drop/0.02, initial measured AIR clearance)`; `transfer_progress=continued_response * measured_transfer * unload` (`semantic_transfer_roles.py:186–215`). Continuation requires real short-window supporting contacts from at least two other legs and an actuated response. Measured redistribution and real initial clearance are intentional alternative routes, not proof that FR itself is carrying load.
- `semantic_supervisor.py:1497–1526`: unplaced RL receives its existing 0.1 unload share from that `transfer_progress` only when the RR-support dependency or already-qualified live RL swing permits it. This contributes at most `0.85/4 * 0.1 = 0.02125` to global potential. Actual reward is `5*(0.9985*Phi_after-Phi_before)` plus the other task/terminal/time terms (`semantic_reward.py:593–599`), **not a standalone positive-dot bonus**, and other components can outweigh it. RL crossing/placement remains separate physical evidence.
- The cooperative workspace term is different: receiver workspace is `0.5*joint_margin_proxy + 0.5*body-relative radial_contraction_response` (`semantic_transfer_roles.py:258`). RL workspace combines edge/receiver/FL-range/RL-corridor weights 0.25/0.5/0.125/0.125 when relevant (`semantic_cooperative_preparation.py:177–186`); this is preparation, not measured FR load reception. Current P12 has neither the front P01/P02 tilt/rate cost nor the P01–P09 collider quality cost. No hip-mount-height or required zero-roll/zero-pitch reward was found in this scope.

## Receiver motion and observations

Within a given window, moving FR's foot toward an unchanged CoM cannot fabricate `dot(delta_COM,d)`: numerator uses only actual CoM displacement, direction uses the old FR position, and receiver displacement is logged separately (`semantic_transfer_roles.py:154–158,240–243`). A moving receiver can change the next sliding window's reference direction, and its body-relative contraction can legitimately increase the separately labelled workspace/preparation proxy; neither is proof of transfer or bearing. If a moving leg changes the measured mass-weighted CoM, that is real CoM motion, still not equivalent to measured support redistribution.

The actor/critic receive role workspace/preparation/transfer progress, motion/readiness, fixed direction x/y, and evidence/response maturity in the inherited 48 role fields (`semantic_observation.py:253–320`), plus world CoM velocity, CoM position relative to base, contacts/loads and body/wheel geometry (`:408–426`). The raw window `delta_COM`, signed lateral progress, and `receiver_world_displacement` are not independent fields in that 48-field group. Detailed logged diagnostic context should not be confused with extra policy inputs.
